The Villa

About This Space

Indulge in the epitome of luxury at our villa nestled in the heart of El Poblado. Boasting elegant, spacious accommodations, a private pool with breathtaking city views, and meticulous attention to detail, our home offers a sanctuary of comfort and style — conveniently located near Medellín's top dining spots and vibrant nightlife.

The villa features 6 beautifully appointed bedrooms with 7 beds in total (4 king-size, 2 queen-size, and 1 double), with air conditioning in every bedroom. Enjoy a private pool and outdoor jacuzzi, spacious living and dining areas, a fully equipped gourmet kitchen, an expansive panoramic terrace, and private on-site parking.

A dedicated on-site team greets you with a warm welcome, a full tour, and is available throughout your stay. Exclusive extras are available on request — private chefs, curated local recommendations, and guided tours to make the most of your time in Medellín.

The Game Plan

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Thu–Sat are the big party nights. Sun–Tue we recover & explore. Everything below is a suggestion — adjust freely.

11Nov
Wednesday

Touchdown & Settle In

Arrival
Afternoon
Arrive at the villa, meet the on-site team, claim rooms.Drop bags, grab a drink, break in the pool & jacuzzi.
Evening
Easy welcome dinner at Ensó or Relato.Both walkable in Provenza, great food, relaxed vibe — no late night, everyone's flown in.
Night
Nightcap on the terrace, plan the week.Save the energy — the party starts tomorrow.
12Nov
Thursday

Comuna 13 by Day, Provenza by Night

Party Night ①
Morning
Sleep in, brunch at Delarte Café.Try the eggs benedict and the crookie.
Midday
Comuna 13 graffiti tour — street art, escalators, history.Go early-ish to beat crowds; ~half day with a guide.
8:00 PM
Dinner + warm-up drinks at Envy Rooftop.Heads up: rooftops here often have a per-group minimum spend — confirm before sitting.
11 PM+
Out in Parque Lleras / Provenza — Vintrash (reggaeton up, techno down).All within a few blocks — easy to bar-hop.
13Nov
Friday

Coffee Country & Club Night

Party Night ②
Late AM
Recovery brunch at Floretto, then pool time.
Afternoon
Coffee farm tour — La Casa Grande (Santa Elena, ~20 min out).Blind tasting + lunch + city views. Mellow, scenic, and short on travel.
8:30 PM
Steak dinner at Ocio (famous short ribs) or Okus.
11 PM+
Big night at Portón de Provenza + La House.La House runs till ~4 AM — multiple floors, multiple DJs.
14Nov
Saturday

Pool Day → The Big One

Party Night ③
Daytime
Full villa pool day. Music, jacuzzi, lazy recovery.Consider a private chef at the villa for lunch (the host offers it on request).
Sunset
Pre-game drinks at Victoria Roof Garden or 360 Rooftop.
9:00 PM
Group dinner — save room, this is the marquee night.
Midnight
Saturday is the biggest night out — Lleras + Provenza clubs.Vintrash goes till ~4 AM Saturdays; pace yourselves.
15Nov
Sunday

Reset & Recover

Recover
Late AM
Slow brunch — Gardenia or back to a Provenza favorite.
Afternoon
Spa & massage day — Element Wellness (cold plunge, oxygen, deep tissue) or MAHAI.Wellness.Book the group in early — these fill up. Best hangover cure going.
Evening
Chill sunset dinner at La Deriva Rooftop, early night.
16Nov
Monday

Guatapé Day Trip

Explore
Early
Drive to El Peñol / Guatapé (~2 hrs each way).Hire a driver or van for the group — easiest with this many people.
Midday
Climb the rock — 708 steps, unreal lake views up top.Bring water & sunscreen; cash for the entry ticket.
Afternoon
Lunch & wander the colorful town of Guatapé — zócalos, tuk-tuks, lakeside.
Evening
Back to Medellín, low-key dinner near the villa.
17Nov
Tuesday

Adrenaline & Farewell Dinner

Explore
Morning
Paragliding in San Félix — fly over the valley (optional, ~45 min out).For anyone not flying: explore Medellín — Botanical Garden, Pueblito Paisa, or Lleras shopping.
Afternoon
Last pool session, pack-prep, souvenirs.
8:00 PM
Farewell dinner at Entre Maderos (city-light views up Las Palmas).Easy last night — flights out tomorrow.
18Nov
Wednesday

Checkout & Fly Home

Departure
Morning
Final breakfast, pack up, settle the Splitwise tab.
Checkout
Hand back keys, airport transfers.JMC (Rionegro) is ~45 min from El Poblado — leave buffer for traffic.

⏱️ Times are loose suggestions. Tours (Comuna 13, coffee, Guatapé, paragliding) are best pre-booked, especially for a group of 6+.
